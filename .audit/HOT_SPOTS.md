## Static Pre-Scan Observations (auditor takeaways)

1. **`unsafe`, `transmute`, raw ptr — zero matches.** Program is pure safe Rust.
2. **Every `as` widening / narrowing in src is checked or bounded.** No silent truncation outside of intended u128 → u64 narrowing where pre-conditions prove safety.
3. **`unwrap()` / `expect()` — zero matches in src.** Only `ok_or` for error propagation. (Tests use unwrap but tests are out of scope.)
4. **No oracle, no Pyth, no Switchboard, no Chainlink.** Confirmed escrow has no external-price surface.
5. **`init_if_needed` — zero matches.** No re-init risk.
6. **All checked_add/sub/mul are paired with `.ok_or(ArithmeticOverflow)?`.** No raw `+/-/*` on money-touching values.
7. **Bundle 1 introduces the only `UncheckedAccount` in a non-destination role (config, in migrate_config).** Manual deserialization is the entire trust boundary for that ix.
8. **Bundle 1 introduces the only non-`has_one`-gated mutating authority ix (ApplyConfigUpdate, AcceptAuthority).** Body-level checks are the entire trust boundary.
9. **Pause does NOT block settle/cancel/reclaim** (H016). Confirmed in both v1 and v2 — settle/cancel paths skip `is_paused` constraint by design.
10. **H023 IncompleteRefund gate present in both files** (v1 L411, L490; v2 L721, L789). Critical mitigation against close=caller rent-sweep.
11. **deposits_mask is u8 (v1, max 4 players) vs u16 (v2, max 10 players).** Type widening matches semantic widening.
12. **Anchor 0.30+ PDA auto-resolution gotcha (per MEMORY.md): callers must NOT pass config or system_program explicitly.** Not a Rust-source-side concern, but the audit should verify all IDL declarations are correct.
13. **Test files have heavy use of `.unwrap()` and `as u64` casts (intentional, scope: src only).** Auditor should NOT chase test-file warnings as production bugs.

The v2 file is roughly 2× denser in HIGH-risk patterns than v1 because of the new Bundle 1 surface (~250 LOC of new code, every line of which involves either authority, time, or money math).
