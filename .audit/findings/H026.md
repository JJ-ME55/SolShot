## Summary

An external actor may send lamports directly to the escrow PDA (via `transfer` or cross-program invocation), inflating its balance. The escrow program has no sanitization for unexpected balance growth. However, the attacker loses money in the process and cannot gain control of the inflated balance.

## Technical Details

**Mechanism:**
- Escrow PDAs hold player wagers (e.g., 0.01 SOL each). 
- A third party can invoke `system_program::transfer` with the escrow PDA as the recipient.
- Solana permits transfers to any account, regardless of program ownership.
- The escrow balance increases; program logic remains unaware of the donation.

**Why NOT a vulnerability:**
1. **Economically irrational**: The attacker LOSES their lamports permanently.
2. **No control over donated funds**: 
   - In `settle_match`, distributed lamports are tied to the `players` array and state machine (lines v1:317–324, v2:434–441).
   - In `cancel_match` and `permissionless_reclaim`, refunds follow the `deposits_mask` bit-set and `remaining_accounts` iteration (no overrun possible).
   - Extra lamports sit in the PDA and are not touched by settlement logic.
3. **Authority can recover donation**:
   - `settle_match` uses `close = authority` (v1:665, v2:697), sweeping leftover lamports to the authority.
   - `cancel_match` uses `close = caller` (v1:718, v2:748), returning unspent balance to the canceller.
   - `permissionless_reclaim` uses `close = caller` (v1:745, v2:773), benefiting the last refundee.
   - Donation is recoverable in all terminal paths.

## Validation

- ✅ No program logic consumes the inflated balance.
- ✅ Settlement splits are hardcoded (90/7/3 BPS, tied to account references).
- ✅ Refund loop is index-and-bitmask controlled, cannot over-run.
- ✅ Lamport conservation is implicit (debit = PDA balance, credit = refunds + rent-sweep).

## Conclusion

H026 is **NOT_VULNERABLE**. Donation attack is economically irrational; attacker loses funds with zero reward.

No remediation required.
