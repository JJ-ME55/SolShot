## Summary

In v2, `start_with_depositors` is gated by a timing requirement: authority must wait until `Clock::get >= deposit_deadline` before calling it. This closes the v1 silent-kick hole (H017) by guaranteeing players a fair deposit window.

However, v2 introduces a **residual economic griefing surface**: the authority's choice of WHEN to call `start_with_depositors` AFTER the deadline determines the final pot size and payout. If authority delays activation strategically, fewer players may have deposited by that moment, reducing the pot and disadvantaging slow depositors.

## Root Cause

**v2:336-339** enforces:
```rust
require!(
    Clock::get()?.unix_timestamp >= escrow.deposit_deadline,
    ErrorCode::DepositWindowNotExpired
);
```

This ensures players have at least `deposit_window_secs` to deposit before anyone can call `start_with_depositors`. But there is NO upper bound on when authority calls it.

**Result**: pot = `wager × count_ones(deposits_mask)`. If authority waits 10 minutes AFTER deadline (when only 2 of 10 expected players have deposited), the pot is `wager × 2` instead of `wager × 10`. The 8 late depositors are silently kicked and forfeit their wagers.

## Attack Scenario

1. Authority creates match: `max_players = 10`, `deposit_window_secs = 60`.
2. Deposit deadline is set to `now + 60s`.
3. 8 players are broadcasting deposits near the 60s mark (slow network, RPC congestion).
4. Authority waits until 60.5s, then immediately calls `start_with_depositors` with only 2 deposits confirmed on-chain.
5. Compaction reduces `max_players` to 2; the 8 pending deposit TXs fail (players no longer in `players[0..2]`).
6. Winner receives `0.45 × wager × 2 = 0.9 × wager` (instead of `0.45 × wager × 10 = 4.5 × wager`).
7. Loser loses `wager` (instead of sharing a larger pot).

Authority profits by keeping wagers while paying out a fractional prize.

## Difference from H017 (v1)

- **H017 (v1)**: No timing gate at all. Authority can call `start_with_depositors` the moment 2+ deposits land, at ANY point after `AwaitingDeposits` is created — locks out all players who haven't deposited yet, regardless of intended window.
- **H033 (v2)**: Timing gate REQUIRES wait until `deposit_deadline`. But authority is not REQUIRED to call it immediately after expiry — they can delay arbitrarily. Slow depositors are locked out, but the ATTACK SURFACE has shifted from "authority kicking players mid-window" to "authority choosing when to activate to minimize pot".

## Invariants Preserved

- State-monotonicity: `AwaitingDeposits → Active` is irreversible once called.
- Refund correctness: non-activated `cancel_match` refunds all deposits correctly.
- No new re-entrancy or CEI violations.

## Severity Rationale

**MEDIUM** (not HIGH) because:
- Authority is a trusted role; this is self-dealing rather than a protocol flaw.
- Players are not silently locked out (v1's worse crime); they can still call `cancel_match` if the match hasn't activated.
- Off-chain governance / smart-contract-wallet controls (e.g., timelock + multi-sig) can enforce fair activation timing.
- This is a **design trade-off**, not a crash or fund-loss bug.

## Recommendations

1. **On-chain upper bound** (future): Add a `max_activation_delay_secs` parameter to `start_with_depositors` — requires authority to activate within X seconds of deadline. Anchor constraint: `Clock::get()? <= escrow.deposit_deadline + max_activation_delay`.
2. **Event logging**: Emit `MatchActive` event with `actual_player_count` and `pot_amount` so off-chain audit can detect skew from expected max_players.
3. **Server policy**: SolShot server should activate immediately after `deposit_deadline` unless explicit delay is authorized (e.g., waiting for a co-op player to join). Document this in match-creation flow.
4. **Token-Economic review**: Verify that 90/7/3 split math is intentional even on fractional pots (see `/H033-pot-scaling.md` if exists).
