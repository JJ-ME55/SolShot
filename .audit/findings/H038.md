## Finding

Solana validator clock drift of 1–2 seconds represents **1.6–3.3% of v2's minimum match duration** (60 seconds), raising the adversarial clock-bidding attack surface from **LOW to MEDIUM** in short-duration scenarios.

## Details

**v2 constants:**
- `MIN_DURATION_SECS = 60` (line 38)
- Drift tolerance: 1–2s per Solana spec
- Drift as % of min duration: 1–2s ÷ 60s = **1.6–3.3%**

**Impact on race windows:**
- Player's `cancel_deadline = match_end_ts` (= activated_at + duration_secs)
- Settlement vs. cancel race occurs after `match_end_ts`
- Clock drift shifts the race boundary by 1–2 seconds
- A losing player observing a settle TX in mempool can submit competing cancel TX
- 1–2s drift could flip the TX ordering in tight deposit windows or at exact deadline boundaries

**Triggerable scenarios:**
1. Exact-deadline cancel (`now == match_end_ts`) — drift causes rejection/acceptance flip
2. Deposit-window boundary (`now == created_at + deposit_window_secs`) — drift allows late deposits
3. Permissionless reclaim boundary (`now == match_end_ts + 24h`) — drift enables front-running by 1–2s

## Threat Model

**Attack:** Adversary monitors short-duration matches (60s–180s). At activation, attempts to race-cancel vs. settle by:
1. Timing cancel submission to exploit 1–2s clock drift window
2. Submitting with higher priority fees just before `match_end_ts`
3. If cancel lands first (due to drift or priority-fee luck), settlement fails; funds refund

**Practical likelihood:** LOW for honest validators (clock drift is random, not adversarial). MEDIUM for coordinated validator-player attack (e.g., player colludes with a validator to set its clock ±1s to advantage their cancel TX).

## Severity Justification

- **Feb (LOW):** Long-duration matches (typical 10–30 min) made 1–2s drift immaterial
- **May (MEDIUM):** 60s minimum + 1.6–3.3% drift overlap with settlement race window — exploitable in adversarial priority-fee scenarios
- **Not CRITICAL:** Impact is ordering flip, not fund loss. Both settle and cancel preserve funds (winner gets payout or refund, respectively)

## Mitigation Path

1. **Short-term:** Document the drift assumption in code comments
2. **Long-term:** Consider slot-based deadlines (immune to clock drift) if off-chain sequencing becomes deterministic
3. **Monitoring:** Log matches with duration <180s; monitor for cancel TXs submitted within 2s of match_end_ts
