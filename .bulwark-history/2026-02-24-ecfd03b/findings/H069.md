## H069 — Room State Leak via broadcastRooms: Partial Exposure

**Verdict:** POTENTIAL

**Severity:** MEDIUM

**Evidence:**
- `server/socket-io/main.js:145-151` — `broadcastRooms()` calls `io.emit('setRooms', { rooms: getOpenRooms() })`
- `io.emit()` broadcasts to ALL connected sockets (no room filtering)
- `getOpenRooms()` returns: `roomId`, `host.name`, `host.color`, `wager`, `matchMode`, `totalRounds`
- Host player names and wager amounts are exposed to every connected player, including unauthenticated observers

**Recommendation:** Filter broadcast to authenticated sockets only, or remove host player names from open room list.
