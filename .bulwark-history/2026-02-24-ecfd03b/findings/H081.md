## H081 — Socket Reconnect 30s Window Abuse

**Verdict:** POTENTIAL

**Severity:** MEDIUM

**Evidence:**
Disconnect handler (main.js ~210-250) sets 30-second reconnect window via `disconnectTimers`. Turn timer continues running during this window: no pause, no extension. At `rejoinRoom` (main.js ~2560+), opponent's turn timer is not reset—their 60-second countdown continues unaffected.

Code snippet (main.js 291): Turn timer fires regardless of reconnect status.

**Attack scenario:** Player in active turn intentionally disconnects at 50s mark. Opponent's timer continues: fires/resumes at 55s. Disconnected player has full 30s to reconnect without their timer advancing, while opponent's timer expires. Opponent loses turn automatically.

**Impact:** Asymmetric advantage during reconnect window. Opponent cannot pause their timer. Attacker gains extra decision time.

**Recommendation:** Either (a) pause all turn timers on disconnect, or (b) extend opponent's timer by disconnection duration, or (c) forfeit turn on disconnect after 10s grace period.
