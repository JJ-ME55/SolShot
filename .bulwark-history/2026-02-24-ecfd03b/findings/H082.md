## H082 — Escrow PDA Reuse After Match

**Verdict:** POTENTIAL

**Severity:** CRITICAL

**Evidence:**
Architecture.md section 5, invariant I-10 notes: "playAgainRequest preserves wagerStates but creates no new escrow PDA."

`server/socket-io/main.js` playAgainRequest handler (line 2498+) copies `wagerStates[originalRoomId]` to new room but does not call `createMatchEscrow()`. Original escrow PDA was closed during first match settlement (Anchor `close` constraint).

When rematch settles, server calls `settleMatch(newRoomId)` which targets the same `wagerStates[newRoomId].escrowPDA` — a **closed, non-existent account**. Settlement TX fails. Retry/cancel also fails. Winner receives nothing.

**Impact:** 100% fund loss for rematch winner. Match result permanently discarded (never written to DB).

**Recommendation:** Require new escrow creation in playAgainRequest: `await createMatchEscrow(newRoomId, ...)` before allowing match start. Enforce fresh PDA per match.
