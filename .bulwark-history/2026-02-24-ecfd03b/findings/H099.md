## H099 — Economic Amplification via Rapid Match Cycling

**Verdict:** POTENTIAL

**Severity:** MEDIUM

**Evidence:**
- server/services/shot-token.js:57-67 — SHOT_MILESTONES include 'first_wagered_match' (10 SHOT) and 'five_win_streak' (40 SHOT)
- Line 59 — first_wagered_match checks: s.wageredMatchesPlayed >= 1 (one-time, fires once per account)
- Line 65 — five_win_streak checks: s.consecutiveWins >= 5 (one-time, fires once per account)
- Practice mode: "all emission rates are 25% of standard" (line 19)
- server/socket-io/main.js:1611-1640 — prestigeBurn triggers SHOT emissions via recordMatchPlayed()

**Analysis:**
Exploit: Create matches with alt accounts, win 5 consecutive practice matches per alt, accumulate SHOT with 25% rate.

Path:
1. Main account (alice) and alt account (bot_1) through bot_5
2. bot_1 plays 5 practice matches against alice: alice wins 5 (five_win_streak = 40 SHOT at 25% = 10 SHOT practice mode)
3. Repeat with bot_2 through bot_5
4. Total: 50 SHOT from 5 bots × 5-win streaks

Concerns:
1. **No daily/weekly cap on milestones** — can re-trigger if streaks reset
2. **Practice mode still rewards** — intended for learning, but rewards accumulation
3. **No wallet-level rate limiting** — no cooldown between streak resets
4. **No match authenticity check** — if both players are bots/alts, streak is artificial

**Recommendation:**
1. Require minimum wallet age (e.g., 1 day) before first milestone
2. Cap milestone frequency: max 1 streak per 24h per wallet
3. Practice mode: disable milestone emissions (line 19 should be 0%, not 25%)
