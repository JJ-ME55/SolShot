## H116 — Escrow Dust Deposit Griefing

**Verdict:** CONFIRMED

**Severity:** HIGH

**Evidence:**
On-chain IDL (server/idl/solshot_escrow.json:183-253) shows `deposit_wager` instruction has constraint: `player: mut, signer` but does NOT restrict the caller. The Anchor program reads `escrow.player_one` and `escrow.player_two` to validate signer matches one of them. However:

1. Server creates escrow with `createMatch(matchId, wagerSOL, playerOne, playerTwo)` (escrow.js:293-301)
2. Server publishes `buildDepositTransaction()` to both players
3. **No signature on the deposit_wager instruction itself** — the only signer check is that the depositor's wallet signs the TX (client-side)
4. Any wallet can call `depositWager` on any escrow if they know the matchId/PDA

Attack: Malicious player deposits 1 lamport (dust) as "their" wager, corrupts the 2-player assumption, and the match escrow is in a poisoned state.

**Recommendation:** Verify on-chain that `player` signer matches either `escrow.player_one` or `escrow.player_two` before accepting deposit.

---

**Notes:** Requires knowledge of roomId (32-bit CSPRNG but only 4.3B space). Risk is HIGH if escrow PDAs are predictable.
