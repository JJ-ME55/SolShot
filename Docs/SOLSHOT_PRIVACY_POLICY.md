## 1. INTRODUCTION

This Privacy Policy describes how SolShot ("we", "us", "the Service") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our game at solshot.gg.

SolShot is designed with privacy in mind. We collect the minimum data necessary to operate the game.

---

## 2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

### 2.1. Information You Provide

**Wallet Address.** When you connect your Solana wallet to SolShot, we receive your public wallet address. This is your sole identifier on the platform. We do not collect names, emails, phone numbers, or any other personal identifying information.

### 2.2. Information Collected Automatically

**Match Data.** We record match results including: participating wallet addresses, wager amounts, weapons used, damage dealt, match duration, winner/loser designation, and settlement transaction signatures.

**Gameplay Statistics.** We track aggregate gameplay statistics per wallet: total matches, wins, losses, win rate, total damage, total Gold earned, SHOT tokens earned, prestige tier.

**Technical Data.** We may collect: IP address (for rate limiting and abuse prevention), browser type, device type, and connection timestamps. This data is not linked to wallet addresses for analytics purposes.

**Cookies.** SolShot uses essential cookies only for session management. We do not use tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or third-party analytics cookies.

### 2.3. Information We Do NOT Collect

- Real names, emails, or phone numbers
- Private keys or seed phrases
- Financial information beyond wallet address
- Location data (beyond IP-derived country for compliance)
- Social media accounts
- Biometric data

---

## 3. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

We use collected information to:

3.1. Operate the game — matchmaking, match execution, and settlement.

3.2. Maintain gameplay statistics and leaderboards.

3.3. Administer SHOT token emissions based on gameplay milestones.

3.4. Prevent cheating, abuse, and prohibited conduct.

3.5. Comply with applicable legal requirements.

3.6. Improve game performance and fix bugs.

We do NOT use your information to:

- Sell to third parties
- Serve advertisements
- Build marketing profiles
- Send unsolicited communications

---

## 4. ON-CHAIN DATA

4.1. Certain actions in SolShot result in on-chain Solana transactions: wager deposits, match settlements, SHOT token emissions, and prestige burns.

4.2. On-chain transactions are public, permanent, and immutable. We cannot delete, modify, or hide on-chain data.

4.3. Your wallet address and transaction history on the Solana blockchain are publicly visible to anyone, regardless of SolShot's privacy practices.

---

## 5. DATA SHARING

We do not sell, rent, or trade your information.

We may share information with:

5.1. **Service Providers.** Hosting providers (for server infrastructure) and database providers (MongoDB Atlas) who process data on our behalf under contractual obligations.

5.2. **Legal Requirements.** If required by law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request, we may disclose relevant information.

5.3. **Safety.** If necessary to protect the rights, safety, or property of SolShot, its users, or the public.

---

## 6. DATA RETENTION

6.1. Match records are retained indefinitely for leaderboard and statistics purposes.

6.2. Technical logs (IP addresses, connection data) are retained for 90 days and then deleted.

6.3. Gameplay statistics are retained as long as the wallet has been active within the past 12 months. Inactive wallets may have statistics archived or deleted.

6.4. On-chain data is permanent and cannot be deleted by us or anyone.

---

## 7. DATA SECURITY

7.1. We implement industry-standard security measures including encrypted connections (TLS/SSL), database access controls, and server-side input validation.

7.2. Wager funds are held in on-chain escrow smart contracts, not on our servers. We do not hold or have access to user funds.

7.3. No system is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of data transmitted to or stored by the Service.

## 8. YOUR RIGHTS

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

8.1. **Access** the personal data we hold about your wallet address.

8.2. **Delete** off-chain data associated with your wallet address. Note: on-chain data and match records required for game integrity may be retained.

8.3. **Object** to processing of your data.

8.4. **Restrict** processing of your data.

To exercise these rights, contact us via Discord or email. We will respond within 30 days.
