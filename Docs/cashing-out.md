# Cashing out your SolShot winnings

When you win a wagered match on SolShot, your prize lands in your wallet as
**SOL** (Solana's native currency). You have three ways to turn that into
something useful.

---

## 💰 1. Gift cards (recommended for amounts under £100)

**The fastest, simplest path.** Pay in SOL, get a gift card code by email
within ~30 seconds. No bank account needed, no KYC for typical amounts.

**How:**
1. In SolShot, open your wallet — tap the address pill in the top-right →
   tap the chevron menu
2. Choose **💰 CASH OUT (GIFT CARDS)**
3. Bitrefill opens — browse 600+ brands (Amazon UK, Steam, Just Eat, Spotify,
   Argos, Uber, mobile top-ups, hundreds more)
4. Pick what you want, click pay with Solana
5. Your wallet handles the payment automatically
6. Code arrives in your email — use it immediately

**Best for:**
- £5–£100 amounts (perfect for typical wagered wins)
- People who want something *useful* (real shopping, not abstract crypto)
- Anyone who hates bank transfers and waiting

**Notes:**
- Bitrefill takes ~1–3% spread on the SOL/fiat rate. Reasonable for what
  they handle.
- Solana network fee is ~£0.0001 (you'll barely notice).
- KYC isn't required under Bitrefill's threshold (around $1,000 worth at
  current rates).

---

## 🏦 2. Bank withdrawal (for larger amounts)

If you want plain cash in your bank, use a regulated off-ramp provider.

**Coming soon to SolShot:** a one-tap "Cash out to bank" button using
MoonPay Sell. Until then, you can do this manually:

1. Send your SOL from your SolShot wallet to a crypto exchange account
   you control (Kraken, Coinbase, etc.)
2. Sell SOL → GBP on the exchange
3. Withdraw GBP to your bank (UK Faster Payments — usually under an hour)

**Best for:**
- £100+ winnings
- Anyone who specifically wants money in their bank account
- Anyone willing to do one-time exchange KYC for a higher off-ramp ceiling

**Notes:**
- First-time CEX setup requires KYC (ID upload, selfie verification)
- Some exchanges have higher off-ramp fees than gift cards
- Bank settlement is usually fast in the UK (Faster Payments) but can take
  1–3 working days for larger amounts

---

## 🔁 3. Send to your own wallet

Already have a Solana wallet — Phantom, Solflare, Backpack? Send your
winnings there.

1. In SolShot, open your wallet menu
2. Choose **EXPORT KEY** to get your private key (write it down somewhere
   safe — anyone with this key controls your wallet)
3. Import into Phantom / Solflare / whichever wallet you prefer
4. From there, use any standard Solana flow — DEX trading, NFT marketplaces,
   sending to friends

**Best for:**
- People already in the Solana ecosystem
- Anyone who wants to use their winnings as actual crypto (yield farming,
  trading, NFTs, etc.) rather than cashing out

---

## Which one should you pick?

| If you want… | Use |
|---|---|
| To buy something on Amazon / Just Eat / Steam | Gift cards (option 1) |
| Real money in your real bank account | Bank withdrawal (option 2) |
| To keep playing crypto stuff | Send to your own wallet (option 3) |
| All three | Why not — your wallet, your call |

## Why we don't run a payout shop ourselves (yet)

We use Bitrefill instead of building our own gift card shop for two reasons:
they're licensed and regulated to do this in the UK, and we don't want to
take on KYC / fiat handling on day one. They handle the boring + risky bits;
you get instant delivery; we earn a small affiliate fee when you redeem.

Longer term, we're building **The Arcade** — a curated in-app shop where you
spend Tickets (earned from playing across all our games, not just SolShot)
on cosmetics, premium passes, and real-world prizes. That's months away.
For V1 launch, Bitrefill is the path.
