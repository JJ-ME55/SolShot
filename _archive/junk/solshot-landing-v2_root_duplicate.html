// INTEL
Common Questions

HOW DOES WAGERING WORK?

Both players deposit SOL into an on-chain escrow before the match. The smart contract holds funds trustlessly. Winner receives 90% automatically. 7% treasury, 3% operations.

CAN THE GAME BE CHEATED?

No. All physics run server-side. Your client sends inputs only (angle, power, weapon). The server computes everything and broadcasts results. Nothing to hack.

WHAT WALLET DO I NEED?

Any Solana wallet. Solflare or Phantom recommended. Connect in-browser — no downloads, no accounts, no emails. Wallet address is your identity.

WHAT IS GOLD?

In-match currency for buying weapons. Start with 1,000. Earn more from damage (+15/HP), kills (+200), round wins (+300). Resets after each match. Never persists.

HOW DO I EARN SHOT?

By playing. SHOT emits from a fixed pool based on milestones — matches played, wins, damage. Can't buy your way to SHOT. 5% pool cap per month.

WHAT IF I DISCONNECT?

30-second reconnect window. If you don't return, you forfeit and opponent takes the pot. If the server goes down, escrow refunds both players automatically.

MINIMUM WAGER?

0.01 SOL. Tiers: 0.01, 0.05, 0.1, 0.25, 0.5 SOL. Practice matches available at zero wager.

WHAT'S PRESTIGE?

Burn SHOT permanently to unlock tiers (Bronze → Diamond). Each tier grants an exclusive weapon. Burns are irreversible — rank is proof of dedication.

READY TO TAKE THE SHOT?

Connect your wallet. Pick your wager. Prove you're the better shot.
