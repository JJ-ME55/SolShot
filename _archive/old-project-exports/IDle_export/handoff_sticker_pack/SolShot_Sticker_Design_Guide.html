§ 01 — BRAND DNA

What SolShot is, in one breath.

SolShot is a turn-based artillery PvP game on Solana — players wager SOL, fight in BO3 duels, and either flex or get cooked. The brand is military propaganda by way of arcade: orange tracer-fire on volcanic blacks, dog-eared callsigns, scanline grit, ego-bait copy. Everyone is a callsign (GRIZZLY-07, VIPER-12). Wins are loud. Losses are louder.

The stickers should feel like they were printed on the side of a tank — stencilled, slightly distressed, declarative. Telegram is where players talk smack mid-match and post Ws after. These stickers are the ammo.

§ 02 — PALETTE

Five colors. No more.

Every sticker uses a maximum of 3 colors from the core palette + the bone/ink for type and outlines. Resist the urge to introduce blues, greens, or pastels. The orange is sacred — it's the tracer round, the muzzle flash, the W.

§ 03 — TYPOGRAPHY

Two fonts. Both free. Both stencilled.

DISPLAY · BLACK OPS ONE · GOOGLE FONTS
VICTORY · COWARD · GG EZ
Used for all sticker text headlines. Always uppercase. Always tight letter-spacing (0.02–0.05em). Add a 4–6px hard black drop-shadow offset down-right for the "stamped onto metal" feel — never a soft blur.
MONO · SHARE TECH MONO · GOOGLE FONTS
CALLSIGN · M-#0A3F7 · 24:00:00
Used for small labels, codes, and HUD-style overlays. Wide letter-spacing (0.2–0.4em). Olive (#C4A65D) for secondary, accent for emphasis.

Never use: Inter, Roboto, system fonts, anything rounded, anything italic, anything cursive. If you need a third weight, use outlined Black Ops One (transparent fill, 4px bone stroke) — that's the only acceptable variation.

§ 04 — VOICE

How SolShot talks.

Brutally short. Confidently rude. Military-flavoured but never cosplay. Imagine a drill sergeant who plays Worms.

VOICE — DO
  • Imperative verbs. "ACCEPT.", "DECLINE.", "FIRE."
  • Calling people out. "DECLINE = COWARD"
  • Numbers as flexes. "742 DMG. AGAIN."
  • Faux-military codes. "TARGET ACQUIRED", "ROUNDS DOWNRANGE"
  • Punctuation as weapon. Periods. Always periods.
VOICE — DON'T
  • Crypto-bro slang. No "WAGMI", "GMI", "frens", "ser".
  • Emoji. Stickers replace emoji; they shouldn't contain them.
  • Hedging. No "maybe", "sort of", "I think".
  • Genuine politeness. Sarcastic politeness is fine, even encouraged.
  • Long sentences. If it's longer than 4 words, cut it.
§ 05 — MASCOT — DESIGN BRIEF

The Commander & the Tank.

SolShot doesn't have a fixed mascot yet — this pack is where you invent one. Two recurring characters, used together or separately:

▸ THE COMMANDER

A grizzled, helmeted operator. Square jaw, scar, sunglasses or eyepatch, cigar optional. Anonymous on purpose — never see both eyes at once. Wears a faded olive jacket with a SolShot patch (the wordmark).

Range of expression: stoic, smug, furious, dead-eyed boredom. Never smiling warmly. The smirk is the upper bound of joy.

Style reference: Metal Gear box-art, Worms cartoony-but-mean, old Soviet recruitment posters.

▸ THE TANK

A small, stout artillery piece — more cartoon than realistic. Stubby barrel, oversized treads, single pivoting cyclops headlight. About the size of the Commander's torso when they're together.

Personality: the tank is the reaction-comedy character — googly when surprised, shaky when scared, grinning when firing. The Commander is the straight man.

Don't: draw it photoreal. Don't add wheels (it's tracked). Don't give it a face on the front of the hull — the headlight IS the face.

FIRST THING TO DO Before drawing any sticker, do one character sheet for the Commander (5 expressions: stoic / smug / angry / dead-inside / yelling) and one for the Tank (5 states: idle / firing / hit / sleeping / celebrating). Send those for approval. Everything else builds on them.

§ 07 — TECHNICAL SPECS

Telegram requirements — these are hard limits.

STATIC STICKERS · 24 of 30

SpecRequirement
Format.webp (preferred) or .png
DimensionsExactly 512 × 512 px. At least one side must be 512.
File size≤ 512 KB per sticker
BackgroundTransparent (alpha channel). No white box.
ColorsRGB
OutlineAdd a 2–3 px white/bone outline around major shapes — Telegram is used on dark AND light themes; outlines guarantee visibility.

VIDEO STICKERS · 6 of 30

SpecRequirement
Format.webm, codec VP9
DimensionsExactly 512 × 512 px. One side must be 512.
Duration≤ 3 seconds, must loop seamlessly
Frame rateUp to 30 fps
File size≤ 256 KB per sticker
AudioNone. Strip audio track entirely.
BackgroundTransparent (alpha channel — VP9 supports alpha)
EXPORT FROM AFTER EFFECTS / BLENDER Render to PNG sequence with alpha → encode WEBM-VP9 with ffmpeg -i frame_%04d.png -c:v libvpx-vp9 -pix_fmt yuva420p -b:v 0 -crf 30 -auto-alt-ref 0 -an out.webm. The yuva420p + -auto-alt-ref 0 flags are required for transparent VP9.

SUBMISSION TO TELEGRAM

Stickers are uploaded via the official @Stickers bot in Telegram. Owner uses /newpack for static or /newvideo for video, sends each file with a single emoji that represents the sticker, names the pack SolShot, and gets a t.me/addstickers/SolShot link.

§ 08 — WORKFLOW

How to actually deliver this.

  1. Day 1 — Character sheets. Commander (5 expressions) + Tank (5 states). Send PNGs for approval before any final art.
  2. Day 2-3 — Utility stickers (01–05). These are highest-frequency in chat; nail them first.
  3. Day 4-7 — Trash-talk + W-flex (06–20). Type-driven, builds on the character work.
  4. Day 8-10 — Propaganda posters (21–25). Most illustration-heavy. Save for when the style is locked.
  5. Day 11-13 — Video stickers (06, 10, 18, 26, 28, 29). Animate the static versions you've already approved. Don't re-design.
  6. Day 14 — Cover (30) + final QA. Test every sticker on Telegram light theme AND dark theme on phone.
§ 09 — WHAT TO DELIVER

The handoff folder.

  • /static/ — 24× .webp files at 512×512, named 01_GG.webp … 30_pack-cover.webp
  • /video/ — 6× .webm files at 512×512, same naming
  • /source/ — editable source files (PSD / AI / Figma / AE) so we can revise without re-drawing
  • /characters/ — character sheets for Commander + Tank, used for future expansions
  • emoji-mapping.txt — the suggested emoji for each sticker (Telegram requires one per sticker on upload)
