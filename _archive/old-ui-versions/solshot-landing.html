// How It Works
Four Steps to Victory
Connect your wallet, pick your wager, buy your weapons, and blow them up. Simple.
01

Connect & Wager

Connect your Solana wallet. Pick a match tier from 0.01 to 0.5 SOL. Your wager locks into an on-chain escrow — trustless, transparent, instant.

02

Arm Up

Spend Gold in the weapon shop. Every player starts with 1,000 Gold. Do you load up on cheap tacticals or save for a single devastating strike?

03

Fire

Take turns firing at your opponent. Adjust angle, power, and weapon selection. Terrain deforms. Positions shift. Every shot is calculated server-side — no cheats, no hacks.

04

Collect

Winner takes 90% of the pot, settled on-chain in under 2 seconds. Earn Gold from damage dealt. Hit milestones to earn SHOT tokens. Burn SHOT for permanent prestige.

// FAQ
Common Questions

How does wagering work?

Both players deposit SOL into an on-chain escrow before the match starts. The smart contract holds the funds trustlessly — neither player nor the server can steal them. When the match ends, the winner receives 90% of the pot automatically. 7% goes to the treasury, 3% to operations.

Can the game be cheated?

No. All physics calculations run server-side. Your client sends only your inputs (angle, power, weapon choice). The server computes trajectories, damage, and terrain changes — then broadcasts the results. There's nothing to hack on your end.

What wallet do I need?

Any Solana wallet works. We recommend Solflare or Phantom. Connect directly in your browser — no downloads, no accounts, no emails. Your wallet address is your identity.

What is Gold?

Gold is the in-match currency used to buy weapons in the shop. Every player starts with 1,000 Gold. You earn more by dealing damage (+15 per HP), getting kills (+200 bonus), and winning rounds (+300 bonus). Gold resets after each match — it never persists and can't be traded.

How do I earn SHOT tokens?

By playing. SHOT is emitted from a fixed reward pool based on gameplay milestones — matches played, wins, damage dealt. You can't buy your way to SHOT. Maximum emission is capped at 5% of the remaining pool per month, creating natural scarcity over time.

What happens if I disconnect?

If you disconnect during a wagered match, you forfeit. The server waits 30 seconds for reconnection. If you don't return, your opponent wins and receives the pot. If the server itself goes down, the escrow refunds both players automatically.

Is there a minimum wager?

Yes. Minimum wager is 0.01 SOL. Wager tiers are: 0.01, 0.05, 0.1, 0.25, and 0.5 SOL. You can also play practice matches with zero wager to learn the ropes.

What's the prestige system?

Burn SHOT tokens permanently to unlock prestige tiers (Bronze through Diamond). Each tier grants access to an exclusive weapon that non-prestige players can't use. Burns are irreversible — your rank is proof of dedication, not a purchase.
