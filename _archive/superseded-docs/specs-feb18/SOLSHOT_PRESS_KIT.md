## WHAT IS SOLSHOT?

SolShot is a browser-based multiplayer artillery game built on Solana. Two players face off across destructible terrain, taking turns to adjust angle, power, and weapon selection to outshoot their opponent. Matches can be wagered with real SOL — the winner takes 90% of the pot, settled trustlessly through on-chain smart contracts.

The game draws inspiration from classic artillery titles (Pocket Tanks, Scorched Earth, Worms) and brings them into the blockchain era with real-money stakes, a tiered weapon economy, and a deflationary token system that rewards dedicated players.

---

## KEY FEATURES

**Real SOL Wagering** — Players wager between 0.01 and 0.5 SOL per match. Funds are held in on-chain escrow and settled automatically. No middleman touches the money.

**Server-Authoritative** — All physics, damage, and economy calculations happen server-side. The client sends inputs only. There is nothing to hack.

**13 Launch Weapons** — From the free Single Shot to the legendary Crazy Ivan (15 random explosions), every weapon has unique physics behavior. No reskins.

**SHOT Token** — Earned through gameplay milestones, not purchased. Burned permanently for prestige tiers. Deflationary by design.

**Prestige System** — Burn SHOT to unlock 5 exclusive weapons and cosmetic tiers (Bronze through Diamond). Your rank proves dedication, not a credit card.

**Destructible Terrain** — Every explosion reshapes the battlefield. High ground, craters, and terrain manipulation weapons create dynamic strategy.

## GAME DESCRIPTION (LONG — 150 WORDS)

SolShot brings classic artillery combat to the Solana blockchain. Two players connect their wallets, pick a wager tier, arm up in the weapon shop, and battle across randomly generated destructible terrain. Every shot is calculated server-side — no cheats, no hacks, pure skill.

Winners receive 90% of the pot, settled on-chain in under 2 seconds through trustless escrow smart contracts. The in-match Gold economy creates round-over-round progression: deal damage to earn Gold, spend Gold on better weapons, and snowball your advantage.

Beyond individual matches, players earn SHOT tokens by hitting gameplay milestones. SHOT is permanently burned for prestige tiers — Bronze through Diamond — each unlocking exclusive weapons unavailable to regular players. Every burn reduces the total SHOT supply forever, creating natural scarcity.

SolShot is skill over spend. A new player with perfect aim beats a Diamond prestige player every time.

## TECHNICAL SPECS

- **Engine:** Phaser.js (client), Express + Socket.IO (server)
- **Frontend:** React
- **Database:** MongoDB Atlas
- **Blockchain:** Solana (Anchor framework)
- **Smart Contract:** On-chain escrow PDA for wager settlement
- **Token:** SHOT (SPL Token, 10M fixed supply, mint authority burned)
- **Settlement:** Integer lamport arithmetic, async mutex locks
- **Anti-Cheat:** Server-authoritative physics, input-only client
